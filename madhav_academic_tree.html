Madhav's Flight to Academic Excellence

"A pilot doesn't avoid turbulence — he addresses it and lands safely."

The Flight Metaphor (Papa's Teaching) —
Just like an aircraft pilot prepares for turbulence ahead to ensure a safe landing, a student must identify weak topics early and address them before the exam. Ignoring turbulence doesn't make it disappear — it makes the crash worse. Paired with this: programme your own mind through daily "इच्छा" — desire stated clearly, repeatedly, in solitude. Both together = the complete success system.
🎓 Madhav's Success System BITS Pilani — Computer Science
⚡ Pillar 1 — Address Turbulence Ahead Academic Weak-Spot Strategy
🔍 Identify Your Turbulence Which topics in your quiz / mid-sem / lab / finals feel unclear or risky?
Write them down. Don't assume "that chapter won't come."
📅 Address It — Don't Postpone Today? Address it today.
Sunday? Address it Sunday.
Ask 2 friends. Ask the prof. Use any channel.
The goal: enough clarity to write something in the exam.
📋 The Turbulence Tracker (Daily Check) Maintain a short list:
① Topic   ② Status: Pending / In Progress / Cleared
Review it every evening. Is my turbulence reducing?
🏆 Goal of This Pillar No topic should be "unknown territory" on exam day.
Even 60% clarity on a hard topic is infinitely better than 0%.
🙏 Pillar 2 — हे मन, तू ये इच्छा कर Mind Programming — Daily Practice
✍️ Write Your Iccha Sentences 10–15 sentences. Examples:
"हे मन, तू ये इच्छा कर की कोई भी turbulent topic तू पूरी clarity से समझे।"
"हे मन, तू ये इच्छा कर की तू किसी से compare मत कर।"
Add your own — specific to YOUR weak areas.
🕯️ The 3 Conditions for Practice ① Ekant — Solitude (no one listening)
② Speak aloud — not just read silently
③ Eyes closed — feel it, don't perform it
🔁 Repetition is the Medicine Say each sentence 2–3 times minimum, daily.
Like a medicine course — 1 day's dose doesn't cure.
Daily repetition → mind accepts → behaviour changes.
🌿 What to Include in Your Iccha List ① Academic: clear every weak topic before exam
② Character: never compare self with others
③ Gratitude: acknowledge Bhagwan's grace daily
④ Custom: whatever YOU want to improve
🌅Madhav's Daily Routine — The Two-Pillar Day
🌄
Morning (5 min) Say your इच्छा sentences aloud in solitude. Set the tone for the day's mind.
📓
After Class (10 min) Note which topic felt unclear today. Add to your Turbulence Tracker.
🛠️
Evening Study Block Pick ONE turbulent topic. Address it — textbook, friend, YouTube, prof. Mark it: In Progress / Cleared.
🌙
Night (5 min) Review tracker: what turbulence reduced today? Repeat इच्छा sentences once more before sleep.
