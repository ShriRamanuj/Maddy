What is Self-Management? ⇧ Index
📄 Raj Jaggi | 25 Jun 2026 — Introduction
🧭 Self-Management
Regulating our thoughts, emotions & behaviour to act sensibly and purposefully in different situations
Made of several interconnected abilities
Self-awareness
Discipline
Emotional control
Adaptability
Time management
Goal-setting
Problem-solving
WHY STRESS FIRST
Stress management is the most important component — excessive stress weakens all the others.
📝 A stressed person may know what ought to be done, yet still fail to think clearly, control emotions or decide well. Fix the stress first, and every other skill starts working.
🎓 You know the whole DSA syllabus, but on exam morning your mind blanks — the knowledge is there, the stress is blocking access.
⚖️ You know Section 16 & the case law cold, but the night before a tough hearing anxiety scatters your argument. Same knowledge, blocked by pressure.
Understanding Stress & Its Universal Nature ⇧ Index
📄 Raj Jaggi | 25 Jun 2026
⚡ Stress
Mental, emotional & physical pressure felt when the DEMANDS on us seem greater than our ABILITY / resources to meet them
SOURCES
Exam, deadline, money trouble, illness, family conflict, competition, criticism, uncertainty about the future
✓ USEFUL (limited)
A little stress helps us prepare, stay alert & respond to an urgent situation
📝 Not all stress is bad — a small dose sharpens you before something important.
✗ HARMFUL when
Excessive  •  Prolonged  •  Creates a feeling of complete helplessness
UNIVERSAL
Cuts across occupation, income, education & status — attendant, sanitation worker, student, homemaker, sportsperson, business leader, PM
📝 The sources and intensity differ, but the human experience of stress is the same for everyone.
NOT A WEAKNESS
Even highly accomplished people face illness, defeat, criticism & setbacks.
Amitabh Bachchan — near-fatal injury during Coolie (early 1980s), a period of professional decline, later serious financial difficulty from a business venture — yet he accepted new opportunities (including television) and rebuilt.
📝 The lesson isn't that he felt nothing — it's that severe pressure can be faced without letting it become the final judgement on your life.
⚠️ WARNING SIGNALS — pause & check honestly
Irritability  •  disturbed sleep  •  headaches  •  physical exhaustion  •  poor concentration  •  loss of appetite  •  constant worry  •  aggression  •  withdrawal from family & friends
🎓 If minor bugs make you furious, assignments cost you sleep, or you're ghosting friends during placement season — that's the body signalling.
⚖️ If a single adverse order ruins your week, limitation dates rob your sleep, or you snap at staff — treat it as a signal to act, not push harder.
Identifying the Cause & Controlling the Controllable ⇧ Index
📄 Raj Jaggi | 25 Jun 2026
🔍 Step 1 — Identify the EXACT cause
We cannot solve a problem that stays vague & undefined
📝 "My whole life is in disorder" is real distress but points to nothing. Break it up: too many commitments + one postponed task + one unpaid bill — now each can be tackled.
💡 Write the worries down — a written problem looks specific & manageable; a fear circling in the head feels endless.
Step 2 — Sort every cause into three buckets
✓ CONTROLLABLE
Take positive action:
• Incomplete work → organise & begin
• Overspending → revise budget
• Misunderstanding → respectful talk
• Skill gap → seek training
◐ PARTLY CONTROLLABLE
Outcome not in your hands, but preparation & response are.
Cricketer can't control pitch, umpire or opposition — can control fitness, focus & conduct
📝 Rohit Sharma couldn't choose when chances came; when asked to open in limited-overs cricket he adapted — and it became a defining stage of his career.
✗ UNCONTROLLABLE
Past events, natural occurrences, another person's decision, the weather, the final result of an exam / interview / match / business call
📝 Accept these calmly — spending energy here only feeds the stress.
ACT — DON'T JUST HOPE
Faith & hope give emotional support but must complement responsible action, not replace it.
Dhirubhai Ambani — modest beginnings couldn't be changed, but he treated them as a starting point, not a permanent barrier, and grew his enterprise on available opportunities.
🔑 Swap the question:
NOT "Why has this happened to me?"  →  ASK "What is the most practical action I can take now?"
🎓 Rejected in one placement round? Uncontrollable = the panel's choice. Controllable = revising your CS fundamentals & projects before the next drive.
⚖️ Can't control how the officer rules. Can control a watertight reply, timely filing & a well-built paper book. Prepare the controllable, release the verdict.
Managing Stress Through Preparation, Time & Action ⇧ Index
📄 Raj Jaggi | 25 Jun 2026
⏳ The real culprit
Much everyday stress comes not from the responsibility itself, but from delay, disorder & insufficient preparation
📝 A task that could be done calmly over ten days turns terrifying when pushed to the final evening.
SET PRIORITIES
Not every activity deserves equal attention. "No time" is often no priorities — hours lost to unplanned chats, social media, TV
✓ REALISTIC SCHEDULE
Clarifies what to do, when to start, how much time exists. Leave room for meals, exercise, family, rest & the unexpected
📝 A timetable that fills every minute becomes a stress of its own. PM Modi's demanding schedule works through planning, delegation & discipline.
✗ PROCRASTINATION
Gives instant comfort but the relief is deceptive — the unfinished task keeps nagging & pressure grows as the deadline nears
REMEDY = small beginning
Not endless thinking. Report → make the outline. Hard subject → study one chapter. Messy house → one room.
📝 A small completed action proves the situation isn't beyond control. Progress builds confidence; confidence makes the next step easier. Self-discipline > waiting for motivation.
PREPARATION
Virat Kohli — fitness, practice & routine show performance isn't talent alone. Prepare before the stressful contest
📝 Preparation can't guarantee victory, but it converts nervousness into alertness. Add contingency: safe documents, buffer travel time, a plan B.
🎓 Semester project due in 3 weeks? Don't marathon the last night. Commit the repo skeleton today (small beginning), timebox modules, keep buffer for the demo that always breaks.
⚖️ 30-day SCN reply window: draft the index & grounds on Day 1, not Day 29. Diarise every limitation date. A calm reply beats a panicked one filed at 11:59 pm.
Protecting the Body & Restoring Emotional Balance ⇧ Index
📄 Raj Jaggi | 25 Jun 2026
🫀 Stress is physical too
It affects breathing, sleep, appetite, digestion, muscle tension & energy — so managing it needs more than positive thinking
✓ MOVE THE BODY
Walk in fresh air, yoga, stretching, cycling, gardening, outdoor games — no gym or gear needed
📝 A short walk after a stressful session won't fix the problem, but it puts you in a calmer, more energised state to face it.
FITNESS + RECOVERY
Virat Kohli — fitness supports concentration, stamina, consistency; he also spoke of mental exhaustion & stepping away to recover.
Leander Paes — decades of tennis = effort balanced with recovery
📝 Strength isn't pretending to have unlimited energy — it's the maturity to know when rest & recovery are needed.
DIET & SLEEP
Regular meals, enough water, reasonable balance. Avoid skipping meals, excess tea/coffee, junk for comfort
📝 A tired mind exaggerates difficulties & reacts impatiently. Rest is a requirement for sound judgement, not a reward earned after all work is done.
BREAKS & PEOPLE
Continuous work ≠ sustained output; attention declines, mistakes rise. A short walk, quiet breathing or a supportive chat restores clarity
📝 But a planned 10-minute break shouldn't become 2 hours of aimless browsing. Time with family is real emotional recovery — sometimes you need advice, sometimes just someone to listen.
🎓 Hostel coding sprint at 3 am fuelled by instant noodles + energy drinks wrecks focus. A morning jog around campus + real sleep debugs your brain faster than caffeine.
⚖️ Filing-season all-nighters with skipped meals lead to careless errors in figures. A disciplined walk, meals on time & 7 hours' sleep protect the very judgement your clients pay for.
Building Resilience & Responding to Failure ⇧ Index
📄 Raj Jaggi | 25 Jun 2026
🌱 Resilience
Some problems — illness, financial loss, professional failure — can't be fixed at once. Resilience = acknowledge sadness, fear & disappointment without letting them define your whole identity or future
📝 Resilience does not demand constant cheerfulness. It needs patience, adaptability & the determination to keep moving forward.
Adaptability in practice
Amitabh Bachchan — rebuilt after accident, uncertainty & financial pressure; success on TV = accepting a different path
Rohit Sharma — delayed success followed patience, changing responsibilities & persistent effort
Jeetendra — long association across changing eras of cinema & TV = treat change as a new reality, not just a threat
How you INTERPRET failure decides everything
✗ SURRENDER
"I have failed; therefore I am a failure" → abandons the possibility of improvement
✓ ACCEPTANCE
"My effort failed on this occasion; I must learn from it & decide what to do next" → recognises reality, invites a wiser response
🎓 A failed course or a rejected internship is one effort that fell short — not a verdict on you as an engineer. Debug the attempt, resubmit, reapply.
⚖️ An order gone against you at the FAA is this round lost, not the war. Study the reasoning, sharpen the grounds & carry it to GSTAT / High Court.
Seeking Support as a Form of Self-Management ⇧ Index
📄 Raj Jaggi | 25 Jun 2026
🤝 True self-management includes knowing WHEN to ask for help
✓ EVERYDAY SUPPORT
Overwhelmed employee → supervisor. Struggling student → teacher. Family / money trouble → counsellor, trusted elder or competent professional
📝 Seeking timely support is NOT abandoning responsibility — it gives you the knowledge, perspective & emotional strength to handle it better.
⚠️ WHEN IT'S SEVERE
If stress seriously disrupts sleep, work, relationships or daily life → consult a qualified doctor or mental-health professional
Persistent hopelessness or panic → needs professional care without delay
📝 Reaching out is a responsible act, not an admission of weakness. Even accomplished people feel anxiety, exhaustion & uncertainty.
THE MINDSET
Public journeys of accomplished people should inspire, not invite unfair comparison. They simply remind us that pressure & setbacks don't remove our ability to seek support, recover and begin again.
🎓 BITS has faculty mentors, seniors & campus counselling — using them during a rough semester is smart engineering, not failure.
⚖️ A second opinion from a senior counsel or a study-circle peer on a knotty matter isn't weakness — it's how good practitioners protect the client & themselves.
From Stress to Strength — The Way Forward ⇧ Index
📄 Raj Jaggi | 25 Jun 2026 — Conclusion
🏔️ Stress is unavoidable — but it need not control our health, judgement, relationships or happiness
The balanced approach — three moves
1️⃣ Honestly identify the cause
2️⃣ Take timely action on what can be changed
3️⃣ Calmly accept what lies beyond control
THE TOOLKIT
Disciplined action  •  careful time management  •  sufficient rest  •  physical well-being  •  supportive relationships  →  face pressure with greater confidence.
🌟 We may not control every circumstance — but we can always strengthen our ability to respond with courage, wisdom and hope.
🎓 Four years at BITS will throw exams, deadlines & rejections at you. The engineer who plans, rests & adapts graduates steadier than the one who only grinds.
⚖️ A litigation career is a marathon of deadlines & adverse orders. The practitioner who manages stress well argues clearer, lasts longer & serves clients better.
